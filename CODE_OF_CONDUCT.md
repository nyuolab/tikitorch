# Community code of conduct

TikiTorch contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation a
harassment-free experience regardless of background, identity, experience level,
or viewpoint.

Be respectful, critique ideas and evidence rather than people, disclose relevant
conflicts, and make room for correction. Harassment, threats, discriminatory
language, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Before
the public community launch, the maintainers must publish and verify a monitored
private conduct-reporting channel distinct from public issues. Until that gate is
closed, coordinate through an existing private channel with the repository owner.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This code applies to repository discussions, reviews, issues, project events, and
public representation of the project.
