The live loop, validated — one process, one 10M-token life

North-star claim: a device sits on its stream; every example it serves, it may also learn from, at about the cost of serving it twice, with no extra silicon, no extra memory class, and no synchronization with anything else. Below: the validation ladder's verdicts, then the V4 life itself — one MorphSSM process serving 10M tokens across five rotated cycles of four domains, learning in place through a margin-gated fused cascade kick.

What is being built — the live loop (D1)

One persistent process on one device. Every token is served; a surprise gate decides — per window — whether the same forward pass also becomes a learning step. There is no training cluster, no second process, no synchronization, no activation-graph memory class. Learning is an occasional property of serving, not a separate activity.

The substrate — a tropical (max-plus) sequence model, trained by cascade predictive coding

Every mixing operation is max/min-plus or a gather: weights enter additively, so at hard routing (τ=0) every parameter gradient is a routed sum of errors — credit assignment needs only gather/scatter/add, never a multiply. The cascade theorem (C1–C3) makes one reverse sweep ≡ fully-settled iterative predictive coding ≡ backprop's gradients, bitwise — so the paradigm content is the schedule, substrate, and audit, never a claim of a better learning rule.

The hypothesis, and what the ladder showed

Hypothesis. If (1) the substrate is tropical — so credit assignment is multiply-free routing over caches the serving forward already produced — and (2) cascade ≡ settled PC ≡ backprop (proven bitwise, G0), then learning can be fused into serving at a marginal cost of one reverse sweep, gated to fire only on genuine novelty. Continuous adaptation then costs almost nothing over a device's life, needs no extra memory class (no activation graph — the substrate trained d832 where autograd OOM'd at 44 GB), and every update is attributable and machine-auditable.

What was measured. The fused loop costs 1.38× a forward in op-volume (V0, bitwise-verified); live learning captures 61–69% of the frozen→oracle headroom on drifting streams (V2); a self-normalized z-gate is provably a fixed-quantile rate limiter and its replacement — the two-timescale absolute margin gate — self-quiets by construction (V2b→V4cal); a tuned LoRA-periodic GPU incumbent needs 3.3× our update budget and a 2.7× memory class to match our quality, while unconditional online BP hurts on stationary data on ANY substrate — gating is universal (V3); and over a 10M-token life the whole stack holds: 60% headroom captured at 1.0063× serving, norm equilibrium, non-increasing interference, sign-drift adversary survived, 0 multiply violations in 1,236 audited kicks (V4).

How novel is this? (adversarial, web-grounded assessment — kept honest)

The components are mostly not novel, and we say so. Tropical/max-plus attention and morphological sequence layers have contemporary parallel work (Tropical Attention, NeurIPS 2025; MorpMamba-class models); matmul-free LMs exist at larger scale; the cascade≡settled-PC≡backprop equivalence is the Z-IL result lineage, not a new theorem; surprise-gated updating and EWC-style precision anchors are established continual-learning tools.

What is ours is the integration and the measurements: (1) the FUSED serve+learn loop — one forward serves and feeds the reverse sweep — built and measured at 1.38× op-volume with bitwise gradient parity; (2) machine-audited zero-multiply TRAINING end-to-end (not just inference), including on real FPGA silicon with bit-exact known-answer tests; (3) the gate law — a self-normalized z-gate cannot self-quiet (fixed-quantile rate limiter), with the absolute-margin gate as the constructive fix, validated on calibration controls; (4) a 10M-token single-process life on the real substrate with retention, norm-equilibrium, and audit evidence; (5) an honest incumbent Pareto in which the segmentation — not a strawman win — is the claim. The two negatives are published with mechanisms: Fisher merging fails under sparse gated firing, and dense substrates reach our text8 quality ~1000× faster (the capability ceiling is real).

Overclaim traps we avoid on purpose: "a better learning rule" (cascade == BP, bitwise — there is no rule-level claim), "first multiply-free training" (anticipated), and "beats GPUs" (only in the low-power/always-on segment, at its measured quality tier).
