The live loop — one process, serving and learning its stream

North-star gate V2, hard-shift regime (gated arm, seed 0). One MorphSSM process walks 800k tokens of a drifting stream: every window is served first (prequential CE, scored before any learning), then a surprise gate decides whether to fire a fused cascade kick on the very caches the serve produced. The frozen twin sees identical windows. Weights update in place; no second process, no synchronization, no extra memory class.
